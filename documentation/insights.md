# Verified business insights

All additive market totals use `PROPERTY_TYPE = 'All Residential'` and periods ending on or before 31 October 2025.

1. **Long-term prices increased 168.83% in a fixed market panel.** Across 256 markets present in both January 2012 and October 2025, the homes-sold-weighted price measure rose from $204,668 to $550,217. The fixed panel reduces the effect of changing city coverage.

2. **The broader October 2025 weighted price was $512,102.** It was 4.51% above October 2024’s $490,014 and 8.10% above September 2025’s $473,733. The month-to-month increase should be read with seasonal caution because the file is not seasonally adjusted.

3. **The project-period sales total is 7,113,013 homes.** This is lower than the 7,361,964 full-file total because the dashboard excludes November 2025–May 2026. Using All Residential prevents subtype overlap.

4. **California, Florida and Texas produced 29.10% of recorded sales.** Their totals were 809,453, 689,987 and 570,637 respectively. This concentration means national-looking totals are strongly influenced by a small group of large states.

5. **October sales activity was below the prior year.** Homes sold fell from 44,933 in October 2024 to 38,893 in October 2025, a 13.44% decline, even while the weighted price measure increased 4.51%.

6. **Inventory fell, but supply still increased.** Inventory declined 11.71% YoY from 152,667 to 134,789, while inventory-weighted months of supply rose 26.40% from 4.72 to 5.97 months. Slower sales can raise supply even when listing inventory falls.

7. **Homes took longer to sell.** The homes-sold-weighted median days on market increased 6.15%, from 49.47 days in October 2024 to 52.52 days in October 2025, consistent with slower transaction activity.

8. **Sale-to-list performance improved modestly.** The weighted sale-to-list ratio rose from 95.48% to 96.42%, an increase of 0.94 percentage points. Sellers still accepted about 3.6% below list price on average in this city-weighted measure.

9. **About one quarter of sales closed above list.** The October 2025 weighted sold-above-list rate was 25.69%, while the inventory-weighted price-drop rate was 30.27%. These measures show that competitive bidding and seller price reductions coexisted across different markets.

10. **Eligible market prices had an unusually wide range.** With a minimum of 20 sales, Newport Beach, CA had the highest October median at $3,849,000, while Johnstown, PA had the lowest at $58,500—a 65.8× difference.

11. **Fast-growth rankings remain volatile at city level.** Westport, CT recorded the largest eligible YoY price increase at 110.32% on 33 sales. The 20-sale rule reduces, but does not eliminate, sensitivity to changes in property mix.

12. **Property types moved differently.** In October 2025, the weighted price measures were $490,777 for single-family homes, $412,892 for condos/co-ops, $466,327 for townhouses and $564,815 for 2–4 unit multifamily properties. These are city-level weighted medians, not transaction-level national medians.

13. **Adding property-type totals would almost double count activity.** Through the cutoff, All Residential contains 7,113,013 sales, while the four subtype totals sum to 7,139,571. Adding both levels would incorrectly report 14,252,584 sales.
